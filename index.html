About

I think about the product before the code. Before writing anything, I ask what problem it actually solves for the person using it — a habit sharpened by industries where getting it wrong has real weight: a missed shipment, a compliance flag, a wrong balance. Logistics, life sciences, and financial services all raised the bar for what "working correctly" means to me.

These days that shows up in how I build with Generative AI and agent systems — retrieval, orchestration, evaluation. Shipping a demo is the easy part. The real work starts after: measuring output quality, catching regressions, and watching how a system behaves once real people are depending on it, the same way I'd treat any other production service.
